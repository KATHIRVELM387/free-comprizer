GOOD TO KNOW

A little clarity.

Small answers to common questions.

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature here is free to use, with no account, subscription, or watermark.

Where do my photos go?

They stay in your browser. The tool doesn’t upload or store your photos on a server. Download any result you want to keep before closing or refreshing the page.

Will it always reach my target size?

Some limits are too small for the dimensions or format you choose. We’ll clearly show whether the result fits. You can allow smaller dimensions, choose JPG or WebP, or increase the limit. Here, 1 KB means 1,000 bytes.

Can I use this for an application form?

Yes. Follow the form’s own format, dimension, and size requirements. This tool can resize and compress a photo; it doesn’t check official photo rules, crop backgrounds, or guarantee acceptance.
